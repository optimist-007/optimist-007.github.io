Materials · devices · sustainability

I ask what a material can do, why it behaves that way, and how that behaviour can become useful.

My work is grounded in experimental materials research across functional nanomaterials, thin films, interfaces and devices. I am interested in energy, sensing and semiconductor-related problems, and I am building computational skills to make future experiments more targeted rather than more trial-and-error.
