BRANCH 01 / ENERGY

Can small, flexible systems make energy more available?

My earlier work with functional nanocomposites and triboelectric devices made me interested in energy beyond large, centralised systems: energy that can be harvested where it is needed and integrated into everyday objects.

A question I am carrying forward: how can energy materials become efficient without becoming resource-heavy?
