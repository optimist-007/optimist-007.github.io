Research map

Questions first. Materials second.

I am interested in functional materials and devices where understanding the mechanism can make the next experiment more intentional.

My strongest base is experimental. I am expanding it with computational materials, DFT and machine learning so that prediction can join the workflow.
Research threads

The themes I keep returning to.

These are working directions rather than a fixed list of keywords. They can move as the questions become clearer.

01 · Functional materials

How do structure and interfaces create useful behaviour?

Interest in materials whose electrical, optical or interfacial properties can be translated into real device functions.

02 · Energy & sensing

Can devices become smaller, simpler and more self-sufficient?

Curiosity around energy harvesting, sensing and systems that can operate closer to where the problem exists.

03 · Semiconductors

What happens when materials thinking meets electronic systems?

A growing interest in semiconductor materials, interfaces and the design constraints that shape practical devices.
